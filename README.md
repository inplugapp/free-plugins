# Free plugins worth having

Free Mac plugins that earn a place on real tracks. Every link goes to the maker's own page.
Got one we missed? Open an issue or a pull request.

Made by [InPlug](https://inplug.app), the Mac app that shows every plugin you own as a picture.

## Reverb and delay
- [Valhalla Supermassive](https://valhalladsp.com/shop/reverb/valhalla-supermassive/): huge reverbs and echoes
- [Valhalla Freq Echo](https://valhalladsp.com/shop/delay/valhalla-freq-echo/): frequency-shifted delay

## EQ and dynamics
- [TDR Nova](https://www.tokyodawn.net/tdr-nova/): dynamic EQ
- [TDR Kotelnikov](https://www.tokyodawn.net/tdr-kotelnikov/): clean mastering compressor
- [Klanghelm DC1A](https://klanghelm.com/contents/products/DC1A.php): two-knob character compressor
- [Xfer OTT](https://xferrecords.com/freeware): the famous multiband squash
- [Venn Audio Free Clip](https://vennaudio.com/free-clip/): clipper

## Color and saturation
- [Softube Saturation Knob](https://www.softube.com/plug-ins/saturation-knob): one knob of warmth
- [ChowTapeModel](https://chowdsp.com/products.html): tape emulation
- [iZotope Vinyl](https://www.izotope.com/en/products/vinyl.html): record noise and wear
- [Analog Obsession](https://www.patreon.com/analogobsession): free hardware-style EQs and compressors
- [Airwindows](https://www.airwindows.com/): hundreds of small, clever effects

## Stereo
- [Polyverse Wider](https://polyversemusic.com/products/wider/): mono-safe widening

## Synths and instruments
- [Vital](https://vital.audio/): wavetable synth
- [Surge XT](https://surge-synthesizer.github.io/): deep hybrid synth
- [Dexed](https://asb2m10.github.io/dexed/): classic FM synth
- [u-he Tyrell N6](https://u-he.com/products/tyrelln6/): analog-style poly synth
- [TAL-NoiseMaker](https://tal-software.com/products/tal-noisemaker): simple, great-sounding synth
- [Spitfire LABS](https://labs.spitfireaudio.com/): free sampled instruments
- [Decent Sampler](https://www.decentsamples.com/product/decent-sampler-plugin/): free sample player

## Bundles
- [Kilohearts Essentials](https://kilohearts.com/products/kilohearts_essentials): 30+ small effects
- [MeldaProduction MFreeFXBundle](https://www.meldaproduction.com/MFreeFXBundle): big free effect bundle

## Meters
- [Voxengo SPAN](https://www.voxengo.com/product/span/): spectrum analyzer
- [Youlean Loudness Meter](https://youlean.co/youlean-loudness-meter/): loudness for streaming
